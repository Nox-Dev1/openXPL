okay but what even is openxpl?


OpenXPL is a Graphics Toolkit project based on scratch. It adds more features to pen.


Documentation


xpl.backgroundhex


xpl.backgroundhex is a function that sets a variable to the desired background colour in hex. Take note that you need to then xpl.cls to actually draw the colour.


xpl.circle

This function lets you draw circles. The inputs are:

Radius

Angle step rate

x y Coordinates of the center of the circle


The radius lets you control... well the radius... Basically the radius is the amount of pixels from the center to the edge of the circle.

The angle step rate lets you control the amount of space between the dots that make up the circle. Lower makes the circle smoother, best for circles with high radius, while higher is less smoother, best for circles with a low radius.

The x and y coordinates define the position of the center of the circle.
